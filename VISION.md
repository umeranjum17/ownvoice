# Ownvoice vision

## Who it is for

People who reply to others and write their own posts, messages and email on their Android phone, and want help that still sounds like them. They are not technical: no jargon, no setup step that needs know-how.

## The problem

Writing help usually lives in another app and sounds like a machine: stock phrases, a voice that is not yours, and ratings that claim more than they can know. You end up copying text back and forth, then fixing it so it sounds like you again.

## Where we are going

A stable app you use every day to reply to people and to create your own posts and content in your own voice, right in the apps you already write in. First on X, then Gmail replies, on your phone.

The everyday flow: Ownvoice reads the post on screen, suggests replies with engagement and stock-wording notes for that platform, you edit, and it inserts the one you pick. You can also speak, polish what you wrote, and send it yourself.

## Principles and quality bar

- **You press Send.** Ownvoice never sends, posts or taps anything for you. Where it cannot confirm the selection, it copies only and never inserts.
- **Your choice of writer.** Private and free on the phone, or with an AI plan you already have. Not locked to one AI service.
- **Honest notes.** Stock wording and engagement are rated separately, and stay honest about what they can predict. Judgment comes from a model and real examples; fixed rules only do mechanical checks.
- **You can see it reading.** A clear animation runs over the part of the screen it reads, and the small interactions feel polished, not plain.
- **It looks good.** Clean, consistent screens in light and dark. A design or flow problem on the main path counts like a crash.
- **The main path works first.** A working journey with rough edges in rare cases beats a perfect one that never ships.
- **Plain words, for anyone.** No technical words on screen, nothing personal baked into the app.
- **Open source**, Apache-2.0.

## What it is not

- Not a bot that posts, sends or replies on its own.
- Not a desktop browser extension or side panel: Ownvoice is mobile first.
- Not for Slack for now.
