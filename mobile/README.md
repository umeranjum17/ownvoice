# Ownvoice mobile app

The Expo app contains the TypeScript core and an Android-only accessibility bridge in `modules/ownvoice-native`. First-run setup includes: a welcome (which downloads nothing), the permission explained kindly (with the animated switch hint, Not now, and the greyed-switch help), a practice chat whose first inserted draft ends the step (the bubble works on this screen only through a practice allowance that is never saved and never overrides pause), and "Where should I help?" offering the installed apps among X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Slack, WhatsApp and Gmail, with the offered bubble choices on by default, followed by an optional ChatGPT sign-in. Setup opens by itself on a fresh install and comes back to the front after the service connects (`ownvoice://setup` deep link); hardware Back finishes setup, while **Not now** skips the permission or optional ChatGPT offer and signing in finishes after app choices are saved; the step plus inserted state survive process death in the kv-store (`src/core/store.ts`, expo-sqlite). The app also has a bubble that reads the focused field and visible screen text on tap, a transparent React Native drafts panel with ChatGPT drafts when chosen or phone drafts through ML Kit GenAI, and Home and settings screens for power, pause, app choices, Your voice and read history. See [ChatGPT integration](#chatgpt-integration) for sign-in, per-app drafting choices and the phone fallback. This app is not yet a replacement for the full experience described in the [main README](../README.md).

The fictional chat with Sam and its empty **Message** field are in setup's **Try it** step. Home shows whether Ownvoice is off, getting ready (with progress), not ready (with **Try again** only while on), paused or ready. Its power switch opens setup to turn the service on in Android settings, or stops the service when turned off. **Where the bubble shows** lists launcher apps with icons, switched-on apps first, a search field and a privacy note. X, LinkedIn, Gmail, WhatsApp, Slack and Reddit have the bubble on by default, even if installed after setup; an explicit off choice stays off. Ownvoice itself starts off outside the practice step. **Your voice** lets you edit never-say phrases, the writing note and two writing rules, or pick or share a markdown file and preview its phrases and rules before **Add these** or **Cancel**. Markdown shares use the same preview and import flow as the file picker; plain-text shares open the rewrite sheet instead. **What Ownvoice read** lists each bubble tap's app, time and help given, without message text; entries older than 30 days are pruned when accessed in the app or when the service next starts or handles an event after a day. **Wipe everything** removes the log, the last captured screen and Your voice. Home also has **Pause for now** and a guidance row for rewriting selected text. Selecting text in another app and choosing Ownvoice, or sharing plain text to it, opens the React Native rewrite sheet without accessibility permission. An empty selection asks you to select text first; otherwise **You selected** marks saved never-say phrases in the original text before any change. Choose **Shorter**, **Simpler** or **Fix spelling** to see a version with phrase marks and a meaning warning if it adds or drops facts; a verdict appears only when the follow-up check has enough evidence. The rewrite follows the stored writing choice: with ChatGPT chosen it is one ChatGPT call under the checks described under [ChatGPT integration](#chatgpt-integration), with this phone still checking the meaning; otherwise this phone writes it. When this phone can't write, only the number check runs, so no verdict appears. Editable selections offer **Replace** (which also copies the rewrite in case the app ignores it) and **Copy** immediately after the rewrite; read-only selections and plain-text shares offer only **Copy**. Chrome may lose its contenteditable selection when the rewrite sheet opens and insert the result at the caret instead of replacing it; paste the copied rewrite if needed. **Fix spelling** leaves a punctuation-free single word without an added full stop. The bubble hides while this sheet is open. Enable the app you want under **Where the bubble shows** to see its bubble. The coral Dot bubble appears only in enabled apps while not paused or showing the panel. It starts at the right edge halfway down; drag it out of the way and it snaps to the nearer edge, remembering a separate spot for each app. When the keyboard opens over a focused field, the bubble moves up if needed to stay above it, then returns to its saved spot. Tap it to get replies when the field is empty, improved versions of text already typed ("Polish your message"), or versions of a new post ("Polish your post"); then choose **Insert**/**Use this** or **Copy**. Replies aim for three slots (agree; kindly disagree; ask the one thing needed); on the phone, a completed reply can appear while the others are still being written, and missing slots get another try. Unusable or repeated replies can leave fewer cards. Duplicate drafts are dropped after normalizing case, punctuation, emoji, whitespace and one leading agreement word; polish rejects versions that flatten paragraph breaks or change list markers. Long dashes are removed unless the writer's text uses one and their no-dashes rule is off. When every shown reply card has the same verdict, its repeated note is hidden; differing verdicts stay beside their cards. **Why?** on any card shows an instant rules row, then the phone model's checks where available, cached per draft. If the other checks cannot run, the cover says its earlier lines came from reading your writing. **Write new ones** reruns with the shown texts excluded; a run with no usable drafts offers **Try again**. The one-time model download starts only after the person agrees to it: picking **On this phone** in setup where the phone still needs it turns **Continue** into **Get it ready** under the sentence "It needs about 2 GB, once, on Wi-Fi", and Home and **How your writing is handled** ask the same way (never while ChatGPT is chosen). The yes is kept under `phone-download-agreed` (`src/core/phoneDownload.ts`); an agreed download resumes when the app opens, runs on Wi-Fi unless the person taps **Use mobile data instead**, and **Free up space** in **How your writing is handled** deletes it and forgets the yes. The file, its pinned SHA-256 and the eligibility check live in the native module. Without the yes, the panel downloads nothing and says **Open Ownvoice to get this phone ready to write.** Once agreed, if the phone needs to prepare its writing model, the panel shows **Getting Ownvoice ready…** with a progress bar when available before switching to **Writing…**; unsupported phones and other failures show a plain-language message only. Insertion checks the field afterward and copies the draft if it fails. The Home screen and drafts panel use the phone's colours, font and light/dark setting; Dot also appears in the panel and launcher icon. The panel closes with the top-right X, Back or a tap outside.

## Checks

On the practice screen the native capture limits conversation to tagged chat lines; elsewhere it excludes Button-class nodes and their descendants, not arbitrary clickable message rows. Captured button labels are removed only when they appear as an exact draft line. `OwnvoiceNative` debug logs record first-token and full-response milliseconds, and the JS log records first-card milliseconds. The stand-in stream test simulates a first card at 2.4 s and a full response at 23 s; these are not real-model measurements.

The ported core checks are in `src/core/__tests__/`; the phone writer has checks in `src/panel/__tests__/`, setup and settings in `app/__tests__/`, and the draft-quality rules in `src/core/__tests__/DraftsTest.test.ts`. The ChatGPT response and switch helpers have tests in `src/chatgpt/__tests__/` and `src/core/__tests__`. Earlier panel emulator evidence: [`reports/OWNVOICE-RN-05.md`](reports/OWNVOICE-RN-05.md). Run the commands above for the current suite.

## Android build and device checks

Run `npx expo prebuild --platform android --no-install`, then build a release APK from `android/` with `./gradlew assembleRelease --no-daemon`. For emulator acceptance only, build with `EXPO_PUBLIC_E2E_STUB=1` (and `EXPO_PUBLIC_E2E_DOWNLOAD=1` for a pretend one-time download, so the ask, the bar and **Free up space** can be walked on an emulator); never use those flags for a distributable build. Build flags are part of Metro's cache key via `metro.config.js`; add any new `EXPO_PUBLIC_*` flag there, and run `sh e2e/flag-cache.sh` to prove a normal export after a flagged one carries no stub code. The local module is discovered from `modules/` by Expo autolinking. Release builds avoid the emulator's shared Metro port. `node e2e/driver.mjs <release-apk> [screenshots-dir]` requires `ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-NNNN`, `adb`, `tesseract` and ImageMagick's `magick` on PATH, and refuses phone serials. It installs the release APK, checks read-back for the React Native field and Chrome textarea/contenteditable, exercises paused/off bubble prevention and service rebind after reinstall, then saves screenshots in the requested directory (by default `e2e/artifacts`). The driver uses screenshot OCR to target controls, adb shell input and window dumps, never UiAutomator (which unbinds accessibility services). Emulators have no phone model, so the flagged build writes three fixed drafts without changing device settings. `node e2e/first-run.mjs <release-apk> [screenshots-dir]` uses the same emulator-only tools and `ANDROID_SERIAL`, needs an APK built with `EXPO_PUBLIC_E2E_GPT=1 EXPO_PUBLIC_E2E_STUB=1` (the offline ChatGPT sign-in stand-in plus fixed drafts), walks setup twice (light and dark) choosing ChatGPT, since an emulator's phone can't write, and saves `light-NN-*`/`dark-NN-*` screenshots; see [`reports/OWNVOICE-RN-06.md`](reports/OWNVOICE-RN-06.md). `ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-NNNN node e2e/settings.mjs [screenshots-dir]` exercises Home, app choices, file import and read history on the installed emulator; see [`reports/OWNVOICE-RN-07.md`](reports/OWNVOICE-RN-07.md). `ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-NNNN OWNVOICE_AVD_NAME=<owned-avd-name> node e2e/rn08.mjs <release-apk> [screenshots-dir]` verifies the target emulator's AVD name before any install or device changes, then checks direct process-text and share intents plus Replace through the selection menu of Your voice's editable field, read-only Copy, the new-number warning and hidden bubble, and can capture the drafts panel's verdict note in light and dark.

Use a throwaway `HOME`, npm cache, Gradle home and AVD home inside the worktree for local validation. The automated driver is emulator-only; phone installs require explicit permission and must not replace the existing app. Do not modify installed-tool configuration.

## ChatGPT integration

**ChatGPT** on Home opens an optional sign-in: tap the button, approve the displayed code on the page that opens, then choose which bubble-enabled apps may use ChatGPT. Slack's ChatGPT choice starts off independently of its bubble setting described above. While a sign-in code is displayed, **Cancel** stops the attempt and returns to the previous screen. Sign-in uses byokit's device-code flow and Expo SecureStore for credentials; sign-out requests revocation and clears the app choices, and starting a new sign-in clears them too. When signed in and ChatGPT has been saved as on for an app, the drafts panel uses streamed ChatGPT Responses as its main writer. Otherwise it writes on the phone; if a request starts but fails, it falls back with a plain failure reason. If sending is stopped before a request starts, it says only that this phone wrote the drafts (or that ChatGPT is turned off). Both writers share the same draft acceptance rules.

**Privacy:** For an enabled app, ChatGPT drafting sends the text being rewritten or replied to, visible screen context and writing rules to OpenAI. Phone-only drafting does not send that content. The read log stores one native tap fact per bubble tap (app, time, what was read and whether a ChatGPT request started), not the screen or message text, for up to 30 days. Checking the public remote switch makes a GET to GitHub without app-added identifiers; the request still exposes ordinary connection information such as the device's IP address to the host. The signed switch accepts newer verified choices, retains the last verified choice if fetching fails, and is checked again before sending; before any verified flag, it defaults to on. If saving a verified choice fails, that choice lasts only until the process restarts; after a restart with no network, an older saved choice may apply. No switch file has been published. A switch permitting ChatGPT never overrides per-app consent. Immediately before starting a ChatGPT request, the app checks its latest sign-in, pause, app choice and switch state; withdrawing consent before this last check keeps drafts on the phone. The selection rewrite sends through the same latest sign-in, pause and switch checks plus its stored writing choice; it rewrites text from any app, so no per-app choice applies to it.

For an opt-in, outside-CI Responses proof using a throwaway copy of an existing sign-in, see [`scripts/live-proof.ts`](scripts/live-proof.ts) and its [recorded result](reports/live-proof.md). Do not pass the installed sign-in file directly or commit credentials.

To produce a switch flag offline, keep a 32-byte private signing key as hex outside this repository and, from `mobile/`, run `SWITCH_SEQ=1 node --experimental-strip-types scripts/sign-switch.ts /path/to/private-key off` (increment the sequence for later flags). This prints the signed JSON; it does not publish it. Never commit the private key.
